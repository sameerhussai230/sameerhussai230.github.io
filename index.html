1. EcoSentinel: Agentic RAG & Multi-Spectral Satellite Pipeline

Geospatial Analytics, EUDR Compliance, AI Agents, Legal RAG

A geospatial pipeline designed to monitor environmental compliance with Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 (EUDR). Integrates Sentinel-2 satellite imagery through the Microsoft Planetary Computer API, calculating multi-spectral indices (NDVI/NDWI) to monitor forest changes. These indices feed into a LangGraph RAG Agent that parses compliance rules to draft structured audit reports.
